# Build expansion skill art

Nine card illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool and converted to WebP for the game. Each used the shared prompt below followed by its subject line.

> Square skill-card illustration for a polished satirical fantasy deckbuilder about a chaotic 1990s software project. Match a painterly editorial cartoon: crisp warm ink silhouette, textured gouache, deep navy shadows, cream paper, teal, coral and antique brass. One strong focal action, high contrast readable at small card size, safe margins for a wide crop. No text, letters, numbers, UI, border, logo, watermark. Subject:

| Game asset | Subject line |
| --- | --- |
| `cards/buffer.webp` | Buffer Exchange: an architect redirects a carefully banked teal shield into one precise brass project lever strike; balance between saved protection and immediate progress. |
| `cards/contingency.webp` | Contingency Plan: a calm architect unfurls a backup blueprint as a coherent teal dome protects the team and a controlled wave pushes three small office monsters back. |
| `cards/triangulate.webp` | Triangulate: a debugger connects three brass diagnostic instruments with glowing teal threads around one hidden clockwork bug, identifying weakness and marking the exact fault. |
| `cards/tracesweep.webp` | Trace Sweep: multiple old computer terminals light up in sequence as one teal scanning beam finds and marks several tiny clockwork defects across the office. |
| `cards/borrow.webp` | Borrowed Weekend: a determined producer reaches into a brass clock and pulls out two glowing extra hours while red overtime slips gather around the desk, energetic but costly. |
| `cards/reallocate.webp` | Reallocate: a producer clears a congested task board, moving red overtime slips into one manageable column while two fresh teal task paths open. |
| `cards/overclock.webp` | Overclock: an old brass computer and office clock surge with teal energy as a hand pushes the speed dial past its safe mark; powerful, desperate tempo. |
