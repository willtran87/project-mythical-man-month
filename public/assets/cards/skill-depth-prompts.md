# Skill depth card art

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then cropped to 768×384 and saved as WebP. No lettering is baked into the art; card names and rules remain live game text.

- `decision-record.webp` — A fantasy office strategist drafts a decision record at a candlelit table, with a quill, parchment, luminous branching plans, and a small architectural model; whimsical painterly adventure card illustration, warm gold and teal, cinematic composition, no text or border.
- `change-freeze.webp` — A magical release seal freezes a stack of change requests while a team braces behind a glowing teal shield; whimsical painterly fantasy office card illustration, warm and cool light, clear focal point, no text or border.
- `backlog-grooming.webp` — A miniature paper backlog is sorted by gloved hands, with one task set aside under a glass bell for tomorrow; whimsical painterly fantasy office card illustration, teal and amber accents, clear focal point, no text or border.
- `migration-plan.webp` — A project team plans a safe crossing between two fantastical office towers over a glowing blueprint bridge; whimsical painterly adventure card illustration, blueprints, lantern light, teal and gold, no text or border.
- `scope-decision.webp` — An executive hand hovers above a map with two diverging routes, one protected by a shield and one racing toward a deadline; whimsical painterly fantasy office card illustration, teal and coral accents, clear choice, no text or border.

The same scenes are shared by related cards where their mechanics tell the same story: Release Cut uses Scope Decision, Pager Duty uses Change Freeze, Archive Ticket uses Backlog Grooming, and Handoff Gate uses Migration Plan.
