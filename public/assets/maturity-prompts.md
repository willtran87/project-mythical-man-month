# Crisis Run expansion art

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final assets were copied into this directory. The Process Auditor and Night Archivist have transparent backgrounds. The Dependency Spider and Metrics Siren rendered with dark gradients despite the transparency requests; both appear on dark encounter panels.

## Process Auditor — `process-auditor.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent enemy character art for a satirical 1990s software-office deckbuilding roguelike
Primary request: The Process Auditor, a stern owl-like bureaucratic creature built from overflowing filing folders, rubber stamps, paper clips, and a tiny necktie. It raises a huge red approval stamp as a shield and peers over round spectacles. Comically officious yet a readable battle threat.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted editorial fantasy cartoon, textured gouache, warm ink outlines, matching whimsical office monster art.
Composition: one complete full-body creature centered, strong silhouette legible at 170 pixels, ample transparent margin.
Palette: cream paper, dusty teal, coral stamp red, mustard brass, deep navy outlines.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background, no setting, no ground, no readable lettering, no logo, no frame, no watermark.
```
