# Crisis Run generated battle art prompts

The generated PNGs are `deadline-dragon.png`, `meeting-mimic.png`, and `burnout-wraith.png`. The game also reuses generated characters and tools from earlier versions; their prompts are recorded in the other `*-prompts.md` files in this directory.

## Deadline Dragon

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent boss character art for a satirical turn-based roguelike browser game about software projects
Primary request: The Deadline Dragon, a theatrical but fearsome dragon made of calendar pages, red overdue stamps, clockwork gears, and tangled ethernet cable. It clutches a giant hourglass with the last grains falling and grins like an executive asking if the release is ready. Memorable final boss silhouette, expressive face, two wings of torn planner pages.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted editorial fantasy cartoon with warm ink outlines and textured gouache, matching whimsical 1990s software office game art.
Composition/framing: one complete creature centered, full body, readable at 200 px, wide enough for wings, generous transparent margin.
Color palette: coral red, mustard brass, dusty teal shadows, warm cream paper, deep navy outlines.
Constraints: truly transparent background, no floor or environment, no legible text, no logos, no frame, no watermark.
```

## Burnout Wraith

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent enemy character art for a satirical turn-based roguelike browser game about software projects
Primary request: The Burnout Wraith, a weary but mischievous little office ghost in a rumpled 1990s shirt, formed from coffee steam, crumpled sticky notes, and late-night desk-lamp glow; one hand holds an empty mug, the other reaches toward a calendar. Slightly spooky, funny, and sympathetic rather than horrific.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted editorial fantasy cartoon with warm ink outlines and textured gouache, matching whimsical 1990s software office game art.
Composition/framing: single full-body specter centered, clear readable silhouette at 170 px, generous transparent margin.
Color palette: ghostly dusty teal, warm cream paper, coral eyes, mustard lamp glow, deep navy ink.
Constraints: truly transparent background, no floor or environment, no legible text, no logos, no frame, no watermark.
```

The generator rendered the Meeting Mimic with a dark background despite the transparency request. It is framed against a dark arena panel in the game.
